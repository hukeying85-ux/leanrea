## 提示词模板

```
Tall vertical 9:20 portrait phone wallpaper. Traditional Japanese ink-brush painting style
(sumi-e) fused with anime character art, warm off-white paper background, generous negative space.
<角色装束 + 动作 + 场景>. Loose expressive ink outlines, visible brush texture.
Full-bleed, no border. Keep the top 15 percent and bottom 12 percent almost empty white paper.
No text, no letters, no calligraphy, no seal stamp, no signature, no watermark, no UI, no clock.
```

关键：**必须写 no calligraphy / no seal stamp**，否则水墨风很容易自己长出题字和印章。
